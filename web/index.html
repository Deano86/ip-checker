    GETTING STARTED
    A closer look, in three steps.
    01
    Connect your providers
    Add all four API keys to your server’s .secrets file. Keep it out of Git and readable only by the account running the checker.
    02
    Choose your addresses
    Paste up to 500 public IPv4 addresses or import a text file. Add one address per line. Comments and duplicates are handled for you.
    03
    Follow the signals
    Watch results arrive, filter the table and open a row for all provider fields. Export the complete scan as CSV for your review.
    Reading your results
    Yes means a positive signal. No is an explicit negative. Unknown means the source did not return usable information.
    Spamhaus PBL alone is not classified as a threat. Registered CIDRs come from RDAP and may differ from routing prefixes. Flags are evidence to investigate, not a safety guarantee.
    Lookup history is saved to the server database and survives restarts. Your administrator sets the retention period. Use Saved results to search and reopen reports. The server allows one active scan at a time.
